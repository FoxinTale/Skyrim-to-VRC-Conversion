For the models:
	Blender 3.4.1 or higher.
	Blender niftools: https://github.com/niftools/blender_niftools_addon/releases/download/v0.1.1/blender_niftools_addon-v0.1.1-2023-11-03-0305c8d5.zip
	Bethesda Archive Extractor: https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/974
	Animation tools: https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/30162
	Robust Weight Transfer (free): https://github.com/sentfromspacevr/robust-weight-transfer/releases 

For the textures:
	Paint.net: https://getpaint.net/download.html#download
	XNormal: https://shadermap.com/home/
	GIMP: https://www.gimp.org/downloads/
	Shadermap Pro (The free option are just fine for us): https://shadermap.com/home/

Other, general tools:
	x/SSE Edit.
	Python, greater than 3.8 installed.
	This entire repository. Otherwise you'll be doing tedious, long, long processes.


This does assume you know the basic controls of Blender (controls), and basic computer knowledge. And know basic anatomy. Yes, I'm serious.
