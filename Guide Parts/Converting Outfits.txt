Fist step - See the guide part on converting textures.

Second, we need a bodyslide preset for the outfit. Otherwise we'll have to make one. That will be a different section of the guide.

Assuming we already have a bodyslide preset, open bodyslide itself, load the preset, make sure "build morphs" is checked. 
While holding left control, click the build button.
If your outfit has multiple parts (pants, shirt, bracelet and such), you will need to do this for each part.
